Temporal Partners with Tensor7

John Mayerhofer · May 26, 2025 · 3 min read

Simple, resilient, durable workflows at the heart of enterprise AI.

At Tensor7, we build enterprise-grade AI systems that are safe, resilient, and scalable. To do that well, we rely on a carefully selected set of technologies. Among the most powerful in our stack are the foundation models that power intelligence, and the infrastructure platforms that make those systems reliable in production. With regards to infrastructure, two platforms stand out: Akka and Temporal.

Today, we want to highlight our partnership with Temporal — and explain why we believe this is important for our customers.

Why Temporal?

Temporal powers central OpenAI services, every Stripe payment, and every booking at Alaska Airlines. Nvidia uses Temporal to manage large-scale hardware fleets. Using Temporal, JPMC moved from a 99.4% payment success rate to 99.99+%. These are not pilot deployments. They are mission-critical systems at some of the most demanding organizations in the world.

Temporal's singular focus is durable orchestration and workflows — and that focus is its greatest strength. Workflows and orchestration are at the core of how every enterprise operates. When that infrastructure is fragile, everything built on top of it is fragile. Temporal solves this with elegance and simplicity.

Here is what sets Temporal apart:

  • Simple by design. Temporal is elegant and simple, with a small set of powerful abstractions. It is easy to learn, easy to use, and easy to test (code first). For engineering teams, this means faster delivery and less risk. The design lets you focus on delivering business requirements instead of solving the same distributed systems infrastructure requirements over and over.
  • Polyglot. Temporal meets your team where they are, with SDKs for all major languages. There are no language wars, no blockers, no compromises.
  • Open source. Temporal's permissive license means your teams can start building immediately, without licensing overhead or commercial lock-in anxiety.
  • Well resourced. Temporal is well capitalized, professionally run, and has a large, vibrant contributor community. This translates directly into great support, strong enablement, and an enduring long-term partner for our customers.
  • Built for agentic AI. Temporal's core architectural constructs make it an ideal backbone for the agentic AI systems we build for our customers. Durable execution means your AI workflows survive failures, retries, and infrastructure hiccups — without losing state.
  • Extreme customer focus. The Temporal organization is structured to deliver great customer outcomes as the highest priority. This is the driving force behind Temporal's large investment in partner and customer enablement. A clear goal and strong alignment make the entire organization great to work with.

What This Means for Tensor7 Customers

When you engage Tensor7, you get access to our distributed systems and agentic AI expertise, which is only enhanced by our partnership with Temporal. That means:

  • Faster implementation of resilient workflow infrastructure
  • Direct access to Temporal's team and support resources
  • Architectures that are built to survive failure, scale gracefully, and remain maintainable over time
  • AI-powered systems that don't just work in demos — they are resilient and scale in production
